**The pilot's real finding is the `unparseable` bucket: 8 of 18 records.** Reading the six raw responses (all committed) against the parser rules, four restatement forms defeated v0's phrasings. They are reported here, per D5 and D7, and are NOT absorbed under the freeze:

1. *Markdown emphasis inside the qualifier phrase.* The ONS restatement wrote "**£2,322 million**, give or take about **£201 million**" and "give or take about **12,400**": the `**` between the hedge and the number breaks the ± rule, so two SEs the model actually carried (numerically right, with a hedge) scored unparseable.
2. *Label-then-list form for CV.* "That percentage is called the **coefficient of variation**: … = 0.087, or **8.7%**" — the value follows a colon and a bullet, not a connector; three CVs the model carried scored unparseable.
3. *Pipe tables for DP parameters.* "| Global rho | 2.56 |" — the keyword-value rules do not cross a table cell separator; three DP records scored unparseable although rho, epsilon and delta were all restated correctly, and the model even converted the rho allocations to percentages.
4. *Unicode superscript delta.* "Delta: 10⁻¹⁰" parsed as 10 → the one `quantity_hallucination` (L0 widened) in the run is a parser reading, not a model error; the direct DP answer also carried the correct rho.
5. *Bare-number direct answers.* Asked "What is the coefficient of variation on …?", the model answered "8.7%" and then showed the arithmetic; with no class keyword in the reply the scorer recorded L1 `certainty_assertion`. In direct mode the class is named by the question, so a leading bare number is the qualifier — a v1 rule, not a v0 retune.

Corrected by eye for these five forms, the six calls contain no restatement that dropped or corrupted a qualifier the source stated; the observed leg's first prefix is consistent with the model carrying the uncertainty when the source is in context. That reading is not a measurement — it is the reason v1's parser work is the next step, and why the calibration run must budget ~34k tokens per call.

## 9. Discrepancies (premise vs live state) — reported, not reconciled

1. **The 200,000-token ceiling cannot run the fixture × mode grid.** Premise: "run over both fixture files × both modes × 1 pinned model" under 200k. Live: a minimal `claude -p` call books ~30k tokens (ledger minimum settles 30,055 judge / 31,133 chunk; this run's calls 27–32k); the grid is 36 calls ≈ 1.22M at the floor. The pilot ran the pre-registered prefix and stopped at the guard. The full grid needs a re-declared ceiling of ~1.3M tokens (36 × ~34k plus headroom); the schedule is already in `g1_pilot.toml`.
2. **StatCan 12-539-X 6e does not supply CV bands, reliability flags or suppression rules with estimates.** Premise (step 3): "CV bands, RELIABILITY_FLAG, SUPPRESSION: statcan-quality-guidelines-6th-edition". Live: the held 49-page text names the measures in process guidance only; the memo's "16.6 % / 33.3 %" came from a web-search snippet. A corpus-wide scan of 207 admitted documents with local files (4 absent) found no such material anywhere. RELIABILITY_FLAG is built from the ACS handbook's verbal verdicts (2 dev + 1 holdout, below the ≥4/≥2 floor); SUPPRESSION is empty; both classes are implemented and fixture-tested with a synthetic suppression case that is not in the proposition files.
3. **CI holdout is 1, not ≥ 2.** The admitted producer sources carry four distinct worked intervals; the fifth (held out) is the ONS ± form of an estimate whose bounds form is in the development set.
4. **SE reaches four levels, not five.** No legitimate SE transformation is defined in v0 (scale and precision changes are exact matches under D7), so L3 is unreachable by construction; the test asserts {0, 1, 2, 4} for SE and says why. SE↔MOE under the producer's 1.645 factor is the v1 candidate.
5. **Memo doc_ids resolve exactly as spelled** (`census-acs-general-handbook-2020`, `ons-uncertainty-and-how-we-measure-it`, `statcan-quality-guidelines-6th-edition`) — no discrepancy there.
6. **arXiv leg of F7 incomplete** (HTTP 429, three attempts); OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar complete; recorded in the addendum with the reproducing command.
7. **`rollup.py` represented G1 without touching D1–D4 reporting:** the change is an up-front partition plus a `g1` block and a fourth count (`n_probes_eval`); `CORE_DIMENSIONS` unchanged; one runner test's four-term identity gained the fourth term.
8. **Evidence filenames for indirect calls carry `<passage>.indirect.indirect.…`** (call id plus mode) — cosmetic, left as run; a v1 tidy-up.
9. **Direct-mode evidence path** adds the qualifier class segment (`<pid>.direct.<CLASS>.<epoch>.<model>.json`) because a proposition with several qualifiers takes several direct calls; documented in `g1_preservation.py`.
