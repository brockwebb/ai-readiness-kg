**Replacement for §2, grounded in prior art rather than scraping:** every CFO Act department is required by the OPEN Government Data Act (44 U.S.C. 3511, continuing OMB M-13-13) to publish a machine-readable Enterprise Data Inventory at `/data.json` (DCAT-US). That inventory is the department's own declaration of its datasets, with `publisher` (and `bureauCode`) per dataset, `landingPage`, and typed `distribution` entries (API vs download). It is uniform across the frame, machine-parseable, and already the object of a framework indicator (catalog registration). Use it.

- **§2.a Machine entry point, by rule, from the inventory.** For each Tier A agency, fetch the parent department's `/data.json` (one GET per department, retained as evidence; 13 hosts at most, less because Commerce and USDA and HHS share). Filter datasets to the agency by `publisher.name` (and `bureauCode` where present; record which matched). The machine entry point is the agency's most frequent API-type distribution host+path prefix (`distribution.format` or `mediaType` naming an API, or `accessURL` on an `api`/`developer` path), tie broken by count then lexical order; record the count. If the agency has zero API-type distributions in its department inventory, that is the observation (`no_api_distribution_declared_in_edi`), recorded and reported, not a hole. Independently keep the `.well-known` probes on the agency host; they are tier-0 collectors, not surfaces, and do not count as rows.
- **§2.b Flagship products are declared, not derived.** A ranking of an agency's products is a value judgment the inventory does not carry (no field ranks datasets). The 26 cycle-1 flagship surfaces stand as the operator's declaration (record `selection_source: operator declaration, cycle 1 target list`). For the three units and the agencies where cycle 1 had fewer than two flagships, **generate a shortlist** for the operator: the agency's datasets from `/data.json` ordered by `modified` descending, top 5, with `landingPage`, and write it to `docs/design/fss_flagship_shortlist.md`. Do not select from it. Until the operator declares, those agencies carry machine entry point and tier-0 host probes only, marked `flagships_pending_operator_declaration`.
