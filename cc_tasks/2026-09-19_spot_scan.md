## 0. What exists and what is missing

`run.py` scans `run.targets(params)`, the whole frame, and names the payload from `params.cycle.name`. Since `2026-09-18_rejudge_seven_legs` every payload records `surface_legs` and `rederive` honours it, so a payload that covers one body's surfaces is already a well-formed payload. The runner cannot yet be asked for one.

Prior art for the shape: incremental and targeted crawls are the norm (Scrapy's start URLs, Lighthouse runs one origin, CIS assessors scan one host); a partial run is a first-class measurement of its subject, not a degraded full one. What must not happen: a spot payload silently standing in for the frame's cycle of record.

**Decisions taken here (operator overrides later):**

1. **`run.py --target <body>` (repeatable) restricts the frame to those bodies' surfaces**; every leg the frame gives those surfaces is run, controls first, exactly as a full cycle. B5 (body-scoped) runs when its body is in the target set. The payload is named `spot_<body>_<YYYY-MM-DD>` (or `spot_multi_…` when more than one), never `scan_…`, and carries `scope: spot` with the target list. `refuse_clobber` applies.
2. **Published through the same `publish.py` path**: Findings to the shard as a new generation of a new cycle, projection, matrices restricted to the bodies present, L0 Results registered `proposed` with the spot cycle's suffix. A spot cycle **never** sets `publication.yaml:snapshot_cycle` and the build refuses if one is named there; a spot cycle is not a `SUPERSEDES` predecessor of any full cycle's Findings and writes no `finding_supersedes`. It stands beside the cycle of record as a later measurement of one body.
3. **Views know about spots.** `get_body(name)` and `score.py --body` report the body's verdicts on the snapshot **and** its latest measurement, whichever cycle that is, named, with the date and a per-leg diff ("passed since the snapshot: D2, A5; still failing: …"). `get_prescriptions(body=…)` ranks by the latest measurement's failures for that body while `bodies_failing_now` (a frame quantity) stays the snapshot's. `get_overview` lists spot cycles separately from full cycles.
4. **The template `cc_tasks/templates/scan_cycle.md` stays the full-frame form.** A second template, `spot_scan.md`, renders with `target` filled and `Network: <that body's hosts>`; `seldon cadence render spot_scan --target bea` or its one-line equivalent is documented. For this machine the direct command is what the operator uses; the RESULT prints it.
5. **The gate for this task runs the spot path end to end on the loopback fixtures**: `--target` naming a fixture body, controls, publish into a throwaway evidence root and event shard, projection into a scratch label set, re-derivation byte-identical, views showing the spot beside the snapshot. Nothing of that run is kept.

**Write set:** `assessment/harness/scan/run.py`, `publish.py`, `rederive.py`, `build_l0_matrices.py` (bodies present), `scripts/score.py`, `scripts/prescriptions.py`, `mcp/` (the three view changes), `docs/reports/build_report.py` (the refusal), `cc_tasks/templates/spot_scan.md` (new), tests, `scripts/check_protected_spot.sh` (new), `seldon_events.jsonl`, the RESULT. `state/`, `corpus/`, `docs/reports/` payloads: byte-identical.
