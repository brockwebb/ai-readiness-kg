**Decisions taken here (operator overrides later):**
1. **The construct is right and the level is wrong.** G1-D asks whether an estimate carries, beside it and in machine-readable form, the fields that make it interpretable: margin of error, standard error, CV, suppression flag, reliability marker. At the host level the surface is a home page, which carries no estimate, so `fail` is structural: every body and every reference host fails, and the column measures nothing about any agency. The operator's restatement of the construct is adopted as its definition: **uncertainty present for the human reader and absent from the markup.** That is the product-tier failure mode the framework exists to name, and it is measured on data products by the January G1 EVAL pilot (`73f0aa5d`, instrument frozen at v2), not by a token match on a front page.
2. **The instrument changes; the history does not.** `G1-D` is removed from `params.tier0.legs` (and from any product-leg list it appears in for the AUTO tier). Cycles 1 to 4 and the self cycle keep every G1-D Observation and Finding on the log under their own `params_hash` and `harness_version` (DD-052 §3, DD-064 §2). **No re-judgement is commissioned.** A re-judgement to `not_applicable` would assert the leg was measurable all along and mis-scored; it was not measurable on that surface, and the record should say so by ceasing to measure it, not by rewriting the verdict.
3. **The framework record tags G1-D `measurement_tier: product`**, via `scripts/framework_writeback.py::save` (the single writer; `--reason` states this task), with the construct sentence from decision 1 on the indicator and a source: the report's own §"Uncertainty fields" paragraph and the G1 pilot's spec. This is the first `measurement_tier` value written; the tiering task under DN-005 §4 item 2 fills in the rest and may rename the field, but it does not remove this one. Projection follows the write-back (DD-057), and `tests/test_framework_projection_roundtrip.py` is green before any Cypher is quoted.
4. **The report re-snapshots on cycle 5, not on this task.** This task changes `params.yaml`, which moves the `params_hash`, which is a new instrument; the next measured cycle is the first under it (DD-060 cadence). Until then the published report stands as it is, one sentence added to its "Uncertainty fields" paragraph by the report builder from a graph fact, not typed: that G1-D was measured at host level in cycles 1 to 4 and withdrawn from the host-level instrument as not applicable to the surface, effective cycle 5, citing the DD this task writes. The L0 matrix builder, `build_l0_matrices.compute`, keeps emitting the G1-D column for any cycle whose params carry the leg and omits it for any cycle whose params do not, so cycle 5's report renders five columns (A4, A5, A10, A11-declared as rated; A12 as candidate) with no code change at that point.
5. **The control fixtures and the derivation.** `fixture_expectations.py` derives expected rows from collector dispatch; removing a leg from the leg list must leave the derivation and the checked-in tables in agreement with no `G1-D` row on the host-level surfaces. A difference is a stop, per DD-061.
6. **DD-066 is written**: G1-D is a product-tier indicator; the host-level leg is withdrawn for cycle 5 forward; history stays as measured; the construct is "uncertainty present for the human reader and absent from the markup." Prior art named in the DD, where a deeper check will test against a standard rather than a token list: SDMX observation-status and confidence attributes; W3C Data Quality Vocabulary; schema.org `Dataset` variable metadata. The DD states these are named as the standards a product-tier rule would bind to and that none has yet been evaluated against a federal data product by this project.
7. **A prescription candidate is recorded, not built.** A ResearchTask (proposed) under DN-005 §2.3: "first sourced Action for the G1 construct: a machine-readable statement (llms.txt or dataset metadata) that points machine readers at the published uncertainty fields and their conditions of use." It carries the operator's wording from 2026-09-15 and no design.
