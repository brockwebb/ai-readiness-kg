## Decisions

1. **Output root `docs/deck/`** (new; `docs/brief/` is the pack's and is written by its generator and nothing else, so the deck lives beside it, not inside it). Files: `docs/deck/brief_deck_content.md` (the content file, the single source of the authored slides), `docs/deck/brief_deck.pptx` (the rendered deck), `scripts/build_brief_deck.py` (renderer plus appendix generator), `tests/test_brief_deck.py`. The content file starts with the same one-line generated/record/cycle comment the pack files carry, naming the pack commit it was built from.

2. **Two kinds of slide, and the renderer knows which is which.** *Authored* slides (chapters A to H, roughly 35 to 45 slides) are written into the content file by this session from the pack pages, under `## Slide N — Title` headers, with a final line `source: <pack file>[, <pack file>]` on every slide that states a number. *Generated* slides (the appendix) are not in the content file at all: `build_brief_deck.py` renders them at build time from `docs/brief/appendix/indicator_<CODE>.md` (one slide per indicator, 49; a sheet that does not fit the 14pt floor is split by the renderer's existing rule, never trimmed), `docs/brief/appendix/rules.md` (one slide per rule, or per rule group if a slide per rule exceeds 40 slides; say which in the RESULT) and a corpus-manifest summary (counts by `source_type` and `decision` from `corpus/manifest.json`, one slide, and a pointer to `C_provenance.csv` for the list; the manifest itself is 264 admitted documents and is not a slide). A generated slide is a projection of a pack file; if the pack changes, the deck changes on the next build, and nothing else.

3. **Chapter order is DN-008 ruling 2, in this sequence, with a cover slide per chapter:** A what it is and why (one page: DN-005 §1, transcribed, not rewritten, plus one slide of "what you are looking at": pack commit, cycle of record, framework record commit, from the INDEX.md header) · B delta against USAFacts (the derivation rules from `B_usafacts_delta.md` §"How the delta is derived", the criteria table, the §8 feedback items quoted whole one per slide, then the indicator table split by criterion, one criterion per slide, from the CSV) · C provenance (the honest coverage line first: 9 of 49 cells located, 27 of 147 edges, 16 indicators without an evidence edge, 82 of 264 documents cited, Title 13, CIPSEA and the SPDs with no admitted document, every figure copied from `C_provenance.md`, then the policy-instrument map) · D using it (the runbook, one command per slide with its captured output from `D_demo_capture.json`, a failed command left in with its failure) · E architecture (the four Mermaid diagrams rendered to SVG or PNG by `mmdc` if installed, else the text rendering the framework deck already uses for diagrams, and the RESULT says which; the "how to read it" text under each) · F results (the pointer page as-is; headline Results by name and id) · G Census dogfood (`G_census_dogfood.md` as-is: 34 failing of 39 judged, hierarchical 0.080, rank 5 of 13, flat 0.190, the one-leg caveat, prescriptions ranked) · H limits and roadmap (`H_limits.md` as-is) · Appendix (decision 2). The "priority order of chapters for a first audience" question is not answered here: the deck is built in ruling order and the question is carried to the operator's read-through in the RESULT, where it belongs.

4. **No number is typed.** Every numeral on an authored slide, after `scripts/numerals.py` strips indicator codes, rule versions, dates, slide numbers, commit hashes and ids, must appear as a `value` in `docs/brief/numbers.json` under one of the pages the slide's `source:` line names, or the build refuses with the slide number and the numeral. This is the pack's own traceability test moved one level up. A number the brief needs that `numbers.json` lacks is a defect in the pack, filed as an Issue, not typed around.

5. **No prose is invented.** Authored slides are transcriptions and condensations of pack prose. Where a slide needs a connective sentence the pack does not have, it is one sentence, it states no fact, and the RESULT lists every such sentence by slide number so the operator's read-through can see what is his and what is glue. Anything that reads as a claim about the world and is not in a pack file does not go on a slide.

6. **Render, then check.** `build_brief_deck.py` renders `brief_deck.pptx`; `--check` re-renders to a temp path and compares byte for byte, the same guard as the pack. `tests/test_brief_deck.py` asserts: idempotence; the numeral gate of decision 4; every `source:` file exists; every authored slide fits the 14pt floor or was split by the renderer, no slide overflows; the appendix slide count equals 49 plus the rule slide count plus one; the framework deck's existing output is unchanged after any refactor of its renderer.

7. **The `kept_verbatim_or_restated` column is measured, not asked.** The pack left it empty for the operator because the record cannot say. The corpus can: the three USAFacts documents are admitted (`usafacts-ai-ready-data-guide`, `usafacts-fde-standards-detailed`, `usafacts-fde-standards-quick-reference`; `corpus/manifest.json` has their paths). Add to `build_brief_pack.py` a locator test for each of the 49 indicators: normalize with `grounding.py`, then test whether the indicator's `construct` string, then its `indicator` string, grounds verbatim in any of the three documents. Fill the column with `verbatim (<doc_id>)`, `restated` (construct or indicator grounds nowhere; criteria A to D only), or `n/a (added criterion)` for E, F and G. Regenerate the pack; only that column and the sentence in `B_usafacts_delta.md` that said the column was left for the operator may change (the sentence now states the test and its counts, which go on `numbers.json`); `--check` must pass afterwards and `scripts/check_protected_brief_pack.sh` must still pass, or the change is wrong. This is the only write to `docs/brief/`, and it is through the generator. Expected outcome, stated so it can be wrong: most or all of A to D come back `restated`, because skeleton line 15 says USAFacts gave no indicator-level tests; if any comes back `verbatim` the RESULT quotes the grounding span and the document.
