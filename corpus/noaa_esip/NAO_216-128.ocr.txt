SUBJECT = NAO 216-128 Artificial Intelligence in NOAA

SECTION 1. PURPOSE.

O01

This NOAA Administrative Order (NAO) establishes the Department of Commerce
(DOC) National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Artificial
Intelligence (Al) Policy. This policy provides high-level direction, guidelines, and
governance to rapidly advance responsible AI use and development to advance mission
and business objectives. Further guidance on how this policy is to be implemented will be
provided in an AI Handbook that governs NOAA AI development and use.

SECTION 2. SCOPE.

.O1

02

.03

This NAO applies to all NOAA AI development and use, including application
development and use for business and administrative workflows, scientific research and
writing, code development, and operations, and to the personnel and organizations that
manage these applications, unless exempted by statutory or regulatory authority.

This NAO applies to all scientific software that contains, relies upon, is developed with,
or enhances discovery using AI.

This NAO does not apply to AI to enhance simple administrative tasks as defined in the-
Handbook.

SECTION 3. DEFINITIONS.

01

Al-Ready Data: Data that is discoverable, machine-readable and
machine-understandable, and has sufficient quality, documentation, and access methods
to support the full AI application development and use life cycle.

Artificial Intelligence (AI): A machine-based system that can, for a given set of
human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations or decisions influencing
real or virtual environments. Artificial intelligence systems use machine and
human-based inputs to: (A) perceive real and virtual environments; (B) abstract such

SECTION 4. POLICY.

02

.03

.06

.08

NOAA shall leverage AI to improve mission and business accuracy, effectiveness,
timeliness, and efficiency, except to the extent limited by law, regulation, policy, contract,
agreement, and security requirements.

NOAA shall maintain the NOAA Center for Artificial Intelligence (NCAI) consistent
with Section 5303 (pp 1180) of the National AI Initiative Act of 2020—Division E of the
2021 National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA), and in alignment with current
Executive Orders to advance the efficient and effective adoption of AI.

NOAA shall maintain a NOAA Artificial Intelligence Executive Committee (NAIEC) to
lead AI approvals and risk management decisions with at least one representative from
each Line Office and representatives from each Staff Office as appropriate.

NOAA shall ensure all AI systems used within NOAA adhere to scientific integrity and
the ethical principles of transparency, fairness, accountability, trustworthiness and the
protection of privacy and civil liberties.

NOAA shall undertake efforts to define, develop, and make publicly available data that is
Al-ready to support efficient and trustworthy AI use.

NOAA shall create and maintain a risk management framework to govern use of AI for
high risk use cases, safeguarding the operational integrity, safety, security and data
provenance of these systems and their training data.

NOAA offices and programs using AI shall establish mechanisms for continuous
monitoring and auditing to assess performance and accuracy.

This Administrative Order shall be reviewed periodically to incorporate advancements in
Al technology, address emerging challenges, and ensure its continued relevance and

effectiveness.

SECTION 5. RESPONSIBILITIES.

O01

The NOAA AI Executive Committee (NAIEC) and NOAA Center for Artificial
Intelligence (NCAI) shall be responsible for managing this Order in consultation with the

appropriate NOAA Councils and Committees.

a. Detailed guidance for the implementation of this Order will be described in the
accompanying Handbook that is aligned with Department of Commerce policies
and directives. The Handbook will be developed and maintained by the NAIEC
with implementation led by the NCAI. The NAIEC will review and update the
Handbook at least every two years or more frequently as needed. The initial and

every two year review of the Handbook will be transmitted to the NOAA
Executive Committee for comments.

.02. The NAIEC shall:

a. Provide governance for, and delegate appropriate authorities to, the NCAI for
strategic implementation of this Order;

b. In coordination with the OCIO, establish and oversee the catalog of approved and
not allowed broadly applicable Al-enabled productivity models, tools, and use
cases in alignment with applicable laws, regulations, and policies;

c. Establish a NOAA AI Risk Management Framework and serve as the AI Risk
Management and Oversight Board;

d. Establish data and metadata standards for Al-ready data in accordance with
applicable NOAA policies and industry best practices;

e. Assist NCAI in project tracking and policy enforcement;

f. Establish and oversee NOAA AI Working Groups consisting of practitioners and
representatives from across NOAA as appropriate

g. The NAIEC Chair shall be responsible for communicating and coordinating with
NOAA Leadership and DOC on the implementation of this policy.

.03 The NCAI shall:

a. Coordinate and seek input and guidance from the NAIEC and appropriate
Councils and Committees;

b. Track and report on active NOAA AI projects to increase transparency, share
tools, insights, lessons learned, and ensure compliance with standards;

c. Actas aconsulting body to NOAA Offices in the procurement process and use of
AI capable software and services;

d. Establish a community of practice for Al-enabled experimentation and application
for NOAA employees and affiliates;

e. Manage, and make available, AI training material and coordinate training
opportunities for NOAA;

f. Develop and maintain guidelines for assessing AI performance, accuracy,
compliance and reporting for monitoring and auditing;

g. Serve as a responsive entity for AI inquiries, providing coordination to ensure
timely responses for NOAA;
