Use DataStream 2 to gather performance and security data for your properties on the global ​Akamai​ edge platform and stream them to a destination of your choice.
With the General Availability (GA) release of DataStream 2.1.1, you can add Edge DNS, EdgeWorkers, and Global Traffic Management as DNS data sources to your streams.
DataStream now supports logging [Security Information and Event Management](https://techdocs.akamai.com/datastream2/docs/security-logs) (SIEM) events generated on the Akamai platform for your security configurations.
With log data streamed in near real-time, you can monitor the delivery performance and health metrics for better insight and improved security of your properties without client integrations or software development kits (SDKs).
Go to [DataStream 2](https://control.akamai.com/apps/data-stream-ui/#/streams/group/all)(login required) in the ​Akamai Control Center​ or use the [DataStream 2 API](https://techdocs.akamai.com/datastream2/reference/api).
