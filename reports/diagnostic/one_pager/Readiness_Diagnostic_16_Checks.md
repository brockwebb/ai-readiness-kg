# Readiness diagnostic: the sixteen checks

*Each check maps to a concern of accessibility in the FCSM data-quality sense (FCSM 20-04).*

Generated by `scripts/sixteen_checks_build.py` from the framework record.

A check is marked Scored when its pass line rests on a published source; four of the sixteen are Scored, read from the instrument's own state and the scoring model rather than typed in.

A check that is not measured always names a reason, and never counts as a fail.
